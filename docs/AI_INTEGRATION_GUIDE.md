# SkillDNA AI Integration & Neural Scoring Guide

## Gemini AI Integration
SkillDNA AI utilizes Google Gemini AI (`@google/generative-ai`) to compute multi-dimensional skill scores:
* **Code Quality** (25% Weight)
* **Problem Solving** (25% Weight)
* **System Design** (20% Weight)
* **Breadth** (15% Weight)
* **Consistency** (15% Weight)
